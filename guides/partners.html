Running Zuri from your phone in 5 minutes a day · v2 · 1 October 2026

Every morning (2 minutes)

  1. Open 📊 Home. Read the 🤖 Zuri manager card: what it noticed overnight.
  2. Read Needs attention — most important first. Tap one to go straight to it.
  3. Glance at Lowest in next 30 days. Red = act now, not on the day.

What the Zuri manager does by itself

When (Kenya time)What happens
06:00 dailyOpens a payment follow-up for each customer whose internet ran out.
06:30 dailyWrites everyone's tasks, early warnings, and a WhatsApp nudge for each staff member.
15:00 dailyChases stuck tasks: over 48h → partners, over 72h → Jerry, over 5 days → urgent.
Friday 16:00Writes the weekly report for the partners.
It only writes tasks, notes, warnings and reports. It never moves money, changes customers, or messages customers. Everything it did is in Tasks → Manager log.

Admin jobs

  • New staff member: they sign up themselves (with their phone) → Me → ⚙️ Admin → People → choose their job and area → switch On → Save.
  • Someone leaves: same place → switch Off. Their work stays.
  • Hide private details (like ID numbers) from some jobs: Admin → Who sees what.
  • Staff & pay: Admin → Staff & pay — everyone you pay, with M-Pesa number, salary and pay day. The daily Pay run (Money → Bills) builds itself from this.
  • The map: Customers → 🗺️ Map, or "Map →" on Home — every customer pinned, by area; red pins ran out.
