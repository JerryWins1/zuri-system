🧑‍🏫 Trainer's Guide: Zuri Training Day

How to train the team and test Zuri together in one morning · v2 · 1 October 2026
Goal: by lunch, every person has watched the videos for their job, done each task once in practice, and written down anything confusing. Practice uses pretend customers — nothing real can break.

Before the day

  • Every phone: open the Zuri link in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  • Sound on, phones charged, one person with good English beside anyone who struggles.
  • Print one quick card per person (Me → 🎓 Learn Zuri → Print & keep).

The morning (about 2 hours)

TimeWhatWho
15 minEveryone together: open Zuri → Open practice → pick your job → Me → 🎓 Learn → Welcome to Zuri and Your task list.Everyone
45 minSplit by job. Watch your job's videos. After each video, do the Your turn list. Tick each one.Techs · Call center · Finance · Partners
30 minThe pretend day (below). Work together like a real day, in practice.Everyone
15 minProblems list: each person reads out what confused them. Write it down (screen, what you tapped, what happened). WhatsApp the list to Jerry.Trainer
15 minReal accounts: each person signs up on the real sign-in page with their phone number. A partner switches them on.Everyone + a partner

The pretend day (in practice)

  1. Call center: a customer, Grace Njeri, calls — no internet. Make a fault job, send it to Peter, tell him on WhatsApp.
  2. Tech: open Grace's job, start it, finish it: found "Bad connector", did "Replaced connector · Tested — working", she paid 1,500 cash.
  3. Call center: work through 3 payment follow-ups: one no answer, one "will pay Friday", one "says paid".
  4. Finance: count the cash, pay the KPLC bill, record 2,000 of hotspot money, copy the Friday report.
  5. Partner: read Home. What is the lowest cash day? Give the tech a task: "Check the splitter at the market".
Practice keeps working on each person's own phone only. People won't see each other's practice work — that's normal. On the real system everyone sees the same jobs.
