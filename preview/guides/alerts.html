📣 Alerts — one-page guide

One message to a whole area or one cabinet · for the office and the partners · v1 · 16 October 2026
Where: 👥 Customers → 📣 Alerts · or the 📡 outage card → 📣 Text the … customers · or the 🗺️ Map → tap a cabinet → 📣 Text the homes on this cabinet
Zuri never sends anything by itself. The office writes an alert, a partner says yes, then the office sends it by WhatsApp or email (free) or the bulk-SMS website (add-on) and taps It went out.

📞 Office: write it (2 minutes)

  1. Tap the code, e.g. 📡 OUT.
  2. Who gets it: By area or By cabinet / tower. Leave Active on.
  3. Fill in what's asked (e.g. when it's back). Add the 🇰🇪 Kiswahili way of saying it: 4pm → saa 10 jioni.
  4. Read What they'll read in both languages. One SMS is up to 160 letters.
  5. Check the count, and anyone left out (said no, no phone, no cabinet).
  6. 📨 Ask a partner for a yes. Urgent? Phone the partner.

⭐ Partner: say yes (1 minute)

  1. Home shows 📣 1 alert waiting for your yes. Tap it.
  2. Read the Kiswahili and the English, and the number of people.
  3. ✅ Yes, approve — after that nobody can change the words or the people. To change it: Cancel and write a new one.

📤 Office: send it

  1. 💬 WhatsApp (free): for each group, 📇 Save contacts → open the file to save them → WhatsApp ⋮ → New broadcast → pick them → 📋 Copy the words → paste → send. (Customers must have saved Zuri's number.)
  2. Small group (30 or fewer)? 📩 Text them one by one from this phone.
  3. ✉️ Email (free): for each batch, 📋 Addresses (BCC) → paste into BCC (never To or Cc) → 📋 Subject and 📋 The words → send from the office email. Send one batch, then the next. The words end with "reply STOP".
  4. 📱 Bulk SMS (add-on): 📥 Download the list → upload it to the bulk-SMS website.
  5. Then ✅ It went out.

When the service is back

Open the outage alert → ✅ Send FIX to the same people → yes → send. Same people, nobody missed.

Each customer's 📣 Messages box (customer page)

  • Wired to: the cabinet or tower. Field techs set it at the install; until then Zuri uses the nearest one on the map. A home with no pin and no cabinet can't get cabinet alerts — Zuri names them.
  • Language: Kiswahili or English.
  • Outages and planned work · Payment reminders: on, unless the customer asks us to stop.
  • Offers and upgrades: only when the customer says yes — and write how and when in the note.
  • ✉️ Emails too: only when the customer says yes — write how and when in the note. Their email goes in ✏️ Edit. They reply STOP? Untick it.

Good to know

  • Quiet hours (19:00–07:00): offers wait for the morning — Kenyan rules allow promotional SMS only 7am–7pm. Outage, service back and power cut may go any time.
  • Every alert keeps its story: who wrote it, who said yes, who sent it, and when — Customers → 📣 Alerts.
  • Partners change the words in 📣 Alerts → Messages, and switch add-ons on, set the SMS price, sender name and quiet hours in Settings.
  • Never promise a time you can't keep. If you don't know, say "today" — and send an update later.
