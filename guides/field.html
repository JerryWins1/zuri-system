🛠️ Field Tech Quick Card

Keep this in the van. Everything a tech does in Zuri · v2 · 1 October 2026
Your three tabs: 🛠️ Jobs (your work) · ✅ Tasks (your to-do list) · 🙂 Me (videos, sync, sign out)

Every job, every time

  1. Open Jobs. Today is at the top. 🔴 = urgent — do it first.
  2. Tap the job → 📞 Call the customer before you go → 🗺️ Open map.
  3. Arrive → tap ▶ I'm starting now.
  4. Done → tap what you found and what you did (the round buttons), or type.
  5. Did the customer pay you? Tap 🙅 No 💵 Cash or 📱 M-Pesa. Type the amount. For M-Pesa, type the code from their message.
  6. Tap ✅ Job finished.
📷 Photos
Before and after. Tap 📷 Add photo. No signal is fine — it uploads later.
🔧 Parts
Write every router, cable and connector you use. Routers: add the serial number.
📍 Map pin
Standing at the house, outside: 📍 Drop pin here. If it says "not accurate", wait a minute and try again.
＋ New problem?
Found something on the road? Jobs → ＋ Log a job.

No signal?

Keep working. Zuri saves everything on your phone. The yellow ⏳ waiting to send badge sends by itself when signal comes back. Don't sign out while things are waiting.

If something goes wrong

  • A red didn't send badge → tap it → Retry. Still red? Screenshot it and WhatsApp Dickson.
  • Can't see a job? It may have been given to another tech. Ask the office.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in page tap Forgot password?
