Payment calls, fault calls, and finding customers · v2 · 1 October 2026
Your tabs: 📊 Home · ✅ Tasks · 🛠️ Jobs · 👥 Customers · 🙂 Me

Every morning: payment follow-ups

  1. Zuri opens a Payment follow-up job for every customer whose internet ran out. Open Jobs.
  2. Open one → 📞 Call.
  3. After the call, tap what happened:
    📵 No answer comes back tomorrow · 🤝 Will pay pick the day · ✅ Says paid we check the next import · ✋ Wants to stop closes it
  4. No answer twice? Tap 📩 Text a reminder (works without WhatsApp) or 💬 WhatsApp it — the message is written for you.
You never close a payment follow-up yourself. It closes by itself when the customer pays.

A customer calls with a problem

  1. Jobs → ＋ New job → type their name or phone → tap them.
  2. Type of job: Fault for no or slow internet. Write what they say, in their words.
  3. Choose the tech and the day (or leave it for the office) → Save job.
  4. Tap 💬 Tell … on WhatsApp → press send in WhatsApp.

New customer wants internet?

Jobs → ＋ New job → leave the customer empty → type New install → put their name and phone in Caller.

The map

Customers → 🗺️ Map: every customer with a pin — green paid up, red ran out. Change the area at the top. Tap a pin: Call, Profile, ＋ Job.

Good to know

  • Never promise a time you can't keep. Say "today" or "tomorrow" — the tech's list decides.
  • Never share another customer's phone number or location.
