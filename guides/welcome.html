Thank you for testing Zuri. Zuri is the one app for the whole Zuri Fiber team: jobs, customers, tasks and money. You will try it on pretend customers and pretend money, so nothing you do is real and nothing can break. We want to know what is confusing, slow, or wrong — that is the whole job.
🧪 Start with the Testers' List: jerrywins1.github.io/zuri-system/testers/
Type your name, pick your job, and it walks you through everything below one step at a time — the videos, the things to try, joining the training copy. Tick each step; write notes to Claude as you go. This page is the long version, for reference.

How to use Feedback Studio (the talking report)

  1. Open the 🎙 Practice inside Feedback Studio link. Zuri appears in the top part; the red button underneath.
  2. Press the red button and just talk while you use Zuri: "this word is strange", "I don't know where to tap", "this is slow". Every sentence is written down with the time.
  3. Something wrong on the screen? Tap 👆 Point, then tap the thing, then say what is wrong. 📸 Screenshot takes a picture.
  4. When you are done: ✅ I'm done — build the report → 📨 Send to Claude → send it to Jerry on WhatsApp or Messages.
Not a talker? The 📝 Note on each checklist line works just as well. Short is fine: "the button is too small".

Good to know

  • Practice stays on your own phone. Me → 🧹 Start over gives you a fresh pretend company any time; 🏗️ Start over (empty company) lets you set everything up from nothing.
  • Me → 🔄 Try another job switches you to another person, so you can see what a tech or the finance team sees.
  • The bottom of every screen shows the version line (for example Zuri System v2.11). Mention it when you report something.
  • The orange bar means TRAINING. The green bar is the real system — not yet.
