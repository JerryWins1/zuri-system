What being a manager looks like here

  • Money. What came in, what went out, what is still owed — without asking anyone.
  • Customers. Who is late, who is about to lapse, who to ring today.
  • Work. Every job, who it is with, what is waiting.
  • Friday. The pay run worked out for you, not from memory.
  • The month. What it actually cost, next to what you expected.

None of that is extra work. It is the same work, written down once, by whoever was already doing it.

Anything confusing, slow or plainly wrong — say so. There is a box on every step for exactly that, and it goes straight to Jerry. A confusing screen is worth reporting even when nothing is broken. That is how this gets better.
